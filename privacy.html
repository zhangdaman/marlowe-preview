Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 6, 2026

Marlowe makes one product: titanium portrait tags, engraved to order. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We've kept it short. If anything is unclear, write us at privacy@marlowe.example.

What we collect

  • Account information: email address (mandatory for sign-in); display name and avatar URL if you sign in with Apple or Google; sign-in provider (magic link / Apple / Google); newsletter consent state and timestamp
  • Contact information: name, phone number (collected at checkout)
  • Shipping address
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe — we never see or store your card number
  • Pet photos you upload through the Designer, used to generate your tag's portrait
  • Designer selections: shape, finish, engraved name, engraved phone number
  • Saved designs and order history linked to your account (so you can reorder later)
  • Basic technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited

How we use it

  • To make and ship your tag
  • To generate your halftone portrait through our AI processing pipeline
  • To email you order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To handle support requests
  • To detect fraud and meet legal obligations (tax records, etc.)

That's it. We don't use your data for advertising. We don't sell it.

Your account & sign-in

Marlowe uses passwordless sign-in. We don't store passwords. You sign in by:

  • Magic link: we email a single-use link valid for 15 minutes
  • Continue with Apple or Continue with Google: standard OAuth — Apple/Google share your email (and optionally display name + avatar) with us based on the scopes you approve

Auth provider: our authentication is operated by Supabase (Supabase Inc., U.S.). Supabase processes session tokens and email-link delivery on our behalf under a Data Processing Agreement.

Why an account is required to generate portraits: AI portrait generation has a real per-call cost. Tying generation to an account lets us prevent abuse without making the experience hostile to real customers, and lets us archive your design so you can reorder it later (every age, every dog, or a backup if the first goes missing).

Account deletion: email privacy@marlowe.example to delete your account. We respond within 30 days. Linked orders are anonymized (we keep the order record for tax compliance — 7 years — but strip your identifiers); saved designs and uploaded photos are deleted; cart is cleared; your auth record is removed from Supabase.

Photos and AI

Pet photos are personal. Here's exactly what happens to a photo you upload:

  • It travels over HTTPS to our servers
  • Our AI pipeline (Replicate or an equivalent processor) converts it into a grayscale halftone portrait suitable for engraving on metal
  • The halftone portrait is stored as part of your order record
  • Your original photo is deleted from our systems one year after your order ships
  • We never use your photos to train AI models — ours or anyone else's
  • We never share your photos with third parties except (a) the AI service that processes them and (b) the engraving operator that produces the tag
One-line summary Your photo is used once, kept for one year, and never sold or used to train models.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — authentication (account creation, magic link delivery, session management)
  • Apple & Google — only if you choose "Continue with Apple/Google" sign-in; they share email + (optional) profile with us
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • AI processing service (Replicate or equivalent) — to generate halftone portraits
  • Manufacturing and fulfillment partner — to engrave and ship your tag
  • SendGrid — to email you order updates and magic links
  • Compliance providers — to detect fraud
  • Government authorities — only if legally required

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Receive a copy of your data ("data portability")
  • Tell us not to "sell" your data — we don't sell it, but you can confirm
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these, email privacy@marlowe.example. We respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first.

Data retention

  • Order records: 7 years (required by U.S. tax law)
  • Original uploaded photos: deleted one year after your order ships
  • Halftone portraits: kept with your order record
  • Account / contact info: until you delete it, or 5 years after your last order

Children

Marlowe is not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you think we have, write us and we'll delete it.

Cookies

We use only what's necessary to keep the site working: session cookies for the cart and basic analytics. We don't use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Updates

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced by email and on this page. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
