§ The leather

Vegetable-tanned. Made to age.

Full-grain cowhide tanned with bark extracts—the way leather was made for 3,000 years before chrome shortcuts. It starts firm and lightly colored, then softens, deepens, and develops patina with every walk.

Hardware is brushed titanium, the same metal grade as our tag. The buckle won't oxidize like brass, won't pit like steel. It outlasts the leather.

  • Leather
    Full-grain veg-tan cowhide, 3–4 mm
  • Stitching
    Hand saddle-stitch, bonded polyester
  • Edges
    Burnished, beeswax-sealed
  • Buckle
    Titanium roller, brushed finish
  • D-ring
    Titanium, welded closed
  • Width
    25 mm (1") all sizes
  • Holes
    7, 25 mm apart, adjustable

How to measure.

Wrap a soft tape (or a piece of string) around the base of your dog's neck where the collar sits. Add about 2 cm (0.75") for a comfortable fit. If your dog is between sizes, size up — leather softens with wear and the buckle has seven adjustment holes.
