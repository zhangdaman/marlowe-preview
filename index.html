§ 03 / The case for slow
A

Each tag is one-of-one.

No two dogs are the same — and no two moments either. Each portrait is generated specifically for the dog and the photo you upload, then crafted, never reused.

B

Better materials. Less waste.

Made-to-order means we don't carry inventory we'll never sell. We engrave on aerospace-grade titanium—lighter than steel, hypoallergenic, will not rust. Each piece is cut from raw stock when you order it, made specifically for this design and no other.

C

Made to outlast.

Titanium, polished to a mirror finish. The portrait is cut as low-relief and halftone—techniques used for fine engraving in metal—so it won't fade or wear off. Order one every year, one for every dog in the house, or one in case the first goes missing — we'll be here.

Our story

It started with a tag that outlived the dog.

My father kept a working Labrador named Mac on a ranch outside Bend, Oregon. When I was eight, he showed me Mac's brass tag—solid metal, name still crisp after fifteen years on a working collar. Mac was long gone. The tag was still in the kitchen drawer.

"I wanted a tag that took the responsibility seriously. I couldn't find one. So we built it."

We make one product. We make it well. Each tag is precision-engraved in titanium, finished in our California atelier. Low-relief and halftone—the same techniques used for fine portraits in metal—so the marks last as long as the metal does. Made to be worn every day. Made to outlive the leash.
