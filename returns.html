Returns Policy

Last updated · May 6, 2026

Each Marlowe tag is engraved to order. There's no inventory, no second copy. Because of that, we treat returns differently than a typical online store: we don't accept returns for change of mind, but we stand behind any tag we make wrong. Read this whole page before you order.

The basics

Custom goods are final sale, with two exceptions:

  • You cancel within 24 hours of placing the order (before production)
  • The tag arrives defective, incorrect, or damaged in transit

Everything below explains how those work.

The 24-hour cancellation window

You can cancel any order, for any reason, within 24 hours of placing it. Email support@marlowe.example with your order number. We'll refund you in full, usually within 3 business days. After 24 hours, your tag enters production and the order is locked.

This is your moment to fix typos, change shapes, or change your mind. Use it. Once that window closes, we can't reverse the engraving.

What we cover after production

If your tag falls into any of these categories, contact us within 14 days of delivery and we'll make it right at no cost to you.

We cover

  • Manufacturing defects (cracks, warping, finish flaws)
  • Engraving errors that don't match what you submitted
  • Wrong shape, finish, or item shipped
  • Damage in transit (open the package within 7 days and tell us)

We don't cover

  • Change of mind after the 24-hour window
  • Typos or errors in what you typed
  • Subjective dislike of the AI-generated portrait (the Designer preview is your final approval)
  • Normal wear, scratches from use, or finish patina over time
  • Loss after delivery

Why "AI portrait dislike" isn't covered

This one needs explaining, because it's where most disputes come from in custom-portrait products.

The Designer shows you a real-time preview of exactly what will be engraved on your tag — same halftone portrait, same shape, same finish, same engraving. What you see in the Designer is what we engrave. If you don't love the portrait, don't proceed to checkout.

Once you've placed an order and the 24-hour window passes, the design is locked. We can't accept returns for "the portrait doesn't look like my dog enough" — by ordering, you've confirmed it does.

How to start a return

If your tag falls under what we cover, here's the process:

  • Step 1. Email support@marlowe.example within 14 days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos of the issue.
  • Step 2. We respond within 1 business day to confirm the issue and authorize a return or remake.
  • Step 3. If a return is needed, we send you a prepaid USPS shipping label. Drop the tag in any USPS box.
  • Step 4. Once we receive it, we inspect the tag and start the remake within 2 business days. New tag ships in 7–10 business days.

Remake first, refund second

For covered issues, our default is to remake the tag at no cost to you. We exist to make every dog get the right tag — not to issue refunds and walk away.

If a remake isn't possible (you no longer want the tag, the same defect happens twice, or we can't fulfill the original order), we issue a full refund through Stripe. Refunds typically clear in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Damage in transit

If your tag arrives damaged, photograph the package and the tag before doing anything else and email us within 7 days of delivery. We'll file the carrier claim and replace your tag — you don't need to deal with USPS or UPS.

Lost packages

If tracking shows your tag as delivered but you can't find it:

  • Check with neighbors, building management, and porch areas
  • Wait 48 hours — sometimes carriers mark items delivered before they actually drop them
  • If still missing, email us. We'll work with the carrier and send a replacement at our cost

Return shipping

  • Our error (defect, wrong item, damage): we pay all shipping
  • Buyer error inside the 24-hour window: no shipping needed (we just cancel before production)
  • Buyer error outside the window: not eligible for return

Refund timing

Once a refund is approved, we issue it through Stripe to the original payment method. Refunds usually appear in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
