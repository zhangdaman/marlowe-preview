Returns & Refunds

Last updated · May 12, 2026

Every Marlowe tag is made just for your dog — engraved with their name, your number, and a portrait we craft from your photo. Because each one is one-of-a-kind, we don't accept returns for change of mind or personal preference. But if something is wrong on our end — blurry, broken, misspelled, lost in the mail — we'll fix it. No back-and-forth, no return shipping, no hassle.

You approve every detail before paying

In the Designer, after we generate your portrait, we walk you through a quick checklist — pet name spelling, phone number, finish, and a final look at the portrait that will be engraved. You'll check off each one and click Approve & Pay. That's your design lock-in.

Take your time on this step. What you see is what we engrave.

24-hour safety window after payment

After payment, you have 24 hours to change your mind. We send you a confirmation email with a Request Redo button. If anything feels off — typo, wrong finish, second thoughts about the portrait — click that button (or just reply to the email). Within 24 hours we'll either redo the design free of charge or refund you in full. Your choice.

If 24 hours pass with no message from you, engraving begins automatically. (We assume "no news = good news" — but the inbox is always open if you change your mind during that window.)

Once engraving begins — it's yours

After the 24-hour safety window closes, the tag is custom-made for your dog and can't be canceled, returned, or refunded. We know this sounds strict — but it's the same reason a tattoo shop can't take back an inked name. Once it's on the metal, it belongs to you and your dog only.

Something wrong with your tag? We'll remake it — free

If your tag arrives with any of these issues, we've got you covered:

  • Engraving is blurry, faded, or misaligned
  • Name or number is misspelled (against the proof you approved? still on us — we'll fix it)
  • Tag arrives damaged, scratched, or dented
  • Color is clearly different from your approved proof
  • Ring, clasp, or hardware is broken

Just snap a photo and email support@marlowe.example within 30 days of delivery. We'll send a new one — no need to return the original. Keep it, gift it, or toss it.

Lost or damaged in shipping? We'll reship — free

Packages get lost. It happens. If yours:

  • Hasn't moved in 14+ days
  • Shows "delivered" but you never got it (please check with your neighbors and household first)
  • Arrives damaged or unsealed

Just email us. We'll remake and reship right away — you shouldn't have to wait on a carrier to investigate for a month.

What we can't do

We want to be upfront, because surprises later aren't fun for anyone. We can't accept returns or refunds for:

  • Change of mind after the 24-hour safety window has closed
  • "It looked different on screen"
  • "I don't love how my dog turned out in the engraving" (the Designer preview + 24-hour window are your two chances to redo)
  • Wanting a different finish or style after engraving has started
  • Wrong info you typed in and approved (please double-check at the approval step)
  • Normal wear, scratches from use, or finish patina over time

This isn't us being difficult — it's the nature of made-to-order. Your tag has your dog's name and your phone number on it. It has zero value to anyone else. That's exactly why we ask you to confirm every detail at checkout, then give you 24 more hours to change your mind.

How refunds work

When a refund is owed (you canceled before approving, or we issue one for an issue we can't fix), we send it back through Stripe to your original payment method. Refunds typically clear in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Need help? Reach out — we're real people

Email support@marlowe.example. We reply within one business day.

When you email us, include:

  • Your order number
  • A photo, if something looks off
  • What's going on, in your own words

That's it. We'll take it from there.

A note from us We're a small team building Marlowe for people who care about their dogs as much as we care about ours. Most issues are easy to fix — and we genuinely want to fix them. Just talk to us.
