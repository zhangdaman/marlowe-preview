Before you write — try these first

Most questions are already answered on one of these pages:

  • "How long until my tag arrives?" — see the timeline on our Shipping Policy
  • "Can I cancel my order?" — each tag is engraved to order, so orders are final once placed. If you just placed it and spotted a problem, email us immediately and we'll try to catch it before engraving begins. See Returns Policy
  • "What if my tag has a defect?" — see what we cover on our Returns page
  • "What happens to my dog's photo?" — see the AI section in our Privacy Policy
  • "Do you ship internationally?" — not yet. See Shipping Policy

Still stuck? Email support. Include your order number if you have one — it speeds things up considerably.
