Shipping Policy

Last updated · May 6, 2026

Each tag is engraved to order in our atelier. We ship to the 50 U.S. states, Washington D.C., U.S. military addresses (APO/FPO/DPO), and U.S. territories. International shipping is not available in this version.

Total: most U.S. orders arrive within 13–19 business days from order placement. We send tracking the day your tag ships.

Cost

Standard shipping is free on every U.S. order. No order minimum, no upcharges, no "handling fees." The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Expedited shipping is not currently offered. Each tag takes 7–10 business days to engrave; faster delivery doesn't make the production faster.

Where we ship

  • All 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C.
  • U.S. military addresses (APO, FPO, DPO) — allow extra time for military mail handling
  • U.S. territories (Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, etc.) — allow 1–2 extra business days

We do not currently ship internationally. If you're outside the United States and want a Mova Craft tag, write us at support@movacraft.example — we're tracking interest for a future expansion.

Carrier and tracking

We ship via DHL Express (or an equivalent international express carrier) with end-to-end U.S. delivery handed off to USPS or UPS for the final mile, depending on your address. The exact route is chosen for the fastest reliable delivery.

You'll receive a tracking number by email the day your tag ships. Tracking updates throughout transit. Any duties or import fees are paid by us — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We ship exactly where you tell us to.

  • If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we'll contact you to confirm the correction. You'll need to pay reshipping costs.
  • If a package is lost because of an incorrect address you provided, we cannot replace it for free.
  • If you spot an error in the address right after ordering, email us immediately — production begins the same business day, so we can only correct addresses if your message reaches us before engraving starts.

Lost or damaged in transit

Lost packages

If tracking shows your tag as delivered but you can't find it:

  • Check with neighbors, building management, and porch areas
  • Wait 48 hours — carriers sometimes mark items delivered before the actual drop
  • If still missing after 48 hours, email support@movacraft.example. We'll work with the carrier and send a replacement at our cost.

Damaged packages

If your tag arrives damaged, take photos of the package and the tag before unpacking further, and email us within 7 days of delivery. We'll handle the carrier claim and replace your tag at no cost. See our Returns Policy for details.

Order tracking

You'll receive emails at three points:

  • Order confirmation — minutes after you place the order
  • Production complete — when engraving is finished and the tag is being packed
  • Shipped — with carrier name and tracking number

If you haven't received an order confirmation within an hour of placing your order, check your spam folder, then email us.

Holiday and high-volume periods

Production timelines may extend by 2–3 business days during U.S. holiday periods (mid-November through end of December) or unexpectedly high-volume weeks. We post advance notice on the homepage when this is in effect.

Contact

  • Order status & shipping questions: support@movacraft.example (include order number)
  • Atelier address: By request only — email support for the current return-receiving address
